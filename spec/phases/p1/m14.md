# M14. Melee

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M2 (weapon), M5 (lag compensation), M13 (bullets).

> **Offline mode was removed on 2026-10-13.** The client no longer simulates the world on its own (no `?offline`, no client-only dummies, no local bullets): without a server it shows a message. The practice range is an online room. Mentions of offline play below describe what was built at the time.

## What it does
**V** throws a punch (with empty hands, left click does too), Apex-style: the rifle drops away and a gloved fist strikes forward. The blow lands after a short wind-up and hurts the **nearest** target that is within reach and in front of you, with no wall in between. One hit per swing; no headshots.

| | |
|---|---|
| Damage | 20 (`meleeDamage`): about 8 swings to take down a full 150 health and shield |
| Knockback | 9 m/s straight away from the attacker plus 2.5 m/s up (`meleeKnockback`, `meleeKnockUp`), only when the blow does damage |
| Reach | 2.4 m, measured from the eye to the nearest point of the target (`meleeRange`) |
| Cone | 55 degrees either side of where you look (`meleeConeDeg`) |
| Wind-up | 0.12 s from key press to the blow (`meleeWindup`) |
| Cooldown | 0.75 s before the next swing (`meleeCooldown`) |
| Lock | no firing for the first 0.35 s of a swing (`meleeLock`) |

## How it is built
- **Shared sim:** the swing timer lives in the weapon state (`meleeT`, `meleeCooldown`) and is stepped by `stepWeapon`, so the client predicts it exactly (`meleeStarted` and `meleeStrike` come out of the step, like a shot). Who it hits is `findMeleeTarget` (`client/src/sim/melee.ts`, Go twin `server/internal/game/melee.go`), locked by `testvectors/melee`.
- **Server:** on the strike the server finds the target using the **rewound** positions the shooter saw (the same lag compensation as a bullet) and applies damage with a `melee` flag on the hit event. Practice targets are hit too; players are not, in the range.
- **Others see it:** the player's entity carries a `meleeing` flag while the swing plays. Other players' bodies drop the rifle to the left hand at the hip and throw the right fist (wind back, strike, return); your own view dips the rifle away and shows a gloved fist punching toward the middle of the screen, with a swing sound and a thud on a hit.
- **Knockback** is applied by the server: players' states get the push (not while mantling or dead), and practice targets get a shove with a hop and a spring that brings them home (wall runners stay on their wall; offline dummies do the same, from `client/src/dummies.ts`). The victim's own client can only learn of the shove from the server, so it arrives about one network trip late and shows up as a prediction correction (measured: about 1.7 m at 160 ms round trip). To make that read as a shove instead of a pop, a melee `hurt` event on you lifts the snap limit (6 m) and blends the correction more slowly (0.16 s) for half a second, and kicks the camera the moment it arrives.
- **Reload** is not touched by a swing: it keeps counting and finishes on time.
- **Offline** practice swings at the local dummies.
- **Viewer:** `/figure.html`, animation "Melee swing" (key V).

## Checked
- Melee vectors (3), a weapon vector for the swing timing and the fire lock, Go tests for the hit after the wind-up, reach and facing, walls, cooldown and fire lock, the entity flag, practice targets, and zero allocations; prediction stays exact (0 corrections) when meleeing online.

## Not done
- No lunge for the attacker, no melee-specific damage on the back or while sliding.
- The knockback cannot arrive sooner than the network allows: the hit event and the victim's new state travel in the same snapshot, so there is nothing earlier to predict from. (The attacker's swing flag does arrive about 100 ms before the blow, but predicting a shove from it would be wrong whenever the swing misses.)
- Numbers are a first guess: playtest the reach, cone and damage.
