# M5. Networked combat

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest in two tabs · **Depends on:** M4, M2

> **Offline mode was removed on 2026-10-13.** The client no longer simulates the world on its own (no `?offline`, no client-only dummies, no local bullets): without a server it shows a message. The practice range is an online room. Mentions of offline play below describe what was built at the time.

## Scope
- [x] Server-side hit detection with lag compensation (about 250 ms position history, rewind to the shooter's view)
- [x] Health + shield with out-of-combat recharge; damage and headshots
- [x] Death, respawn after 3 s, spawn point farthest from enemies and out of line of sight, 2 s spawn protection (ends on fire)
- [x] Kill heals health and shield to full
- [x] Kill feed

## Done when
Two players can fight; hits register fairly at 80 ms; dying and respawning works.

## What was built
- **Combat math on the server** (`server/internal/game/weapon.go`, `hitscan.go`): line-for-line Go ports of the TS weapon and hitscan code, locked to it by shared vectors (`testvectors/weapon`, `testvectors/hitscan`, 7 and 3 scenarios, recorded by `pnpm vectors`). The seeded random generator matches bit for bit, so a shot's spread is identical on both sides.
- **The weapon is part of the simulation**: inputs now carry `fire`, `reload` and `viewTick`. The server runs movement then the weapon for every input it consumes, so the client predicts shots (tracer, ammo, recoil kick) exactly. Snapshots carry the weapon state, and the predictor reconciles it like movement.
- **Lag compensation** (`combat.go`): the server keeps 32 ticks of every player's position. A shot is traced against the other players as the shooter's screen showed them: at the client's claimed `viewTick`, interpolated between the two bracketing snapshot ticks exactly like the client's interpolation. The rewind is capped at 20 ticks (333 ms; see below) and a view tick from the future is ignored. Shooters are not rewound.
- **Health and damage**: 100 health + 50 shield; shield absorbs first. Headshot = the top 0.3 m of the target (so crouched and sliding players have a head too), 1.5x. Shield recharges 20/s after 3 s without damage.
- **Death and respawn**: dead players still consume inputs (so acks flow) but nothing else. After 3 s the server brings them back at the spawn farthest from living enemies, strongly preferring spawns no enemy can see; 2 s of spawn protection (bullets are absorbed without damage; firing ends it early). A kill restores the killer to full health and shield. P is now an instant "new life" dev shortcut.
- **Events**: `hit` (to the shooter), `hurt` (to the victim), `kill` (to everyone), resent in the snapshots of the next 6 ticks and de-duplicated by per-recipient sequence number, so one lost snapshot does not lose a kill.
- **Client**: server-confirmed hit markers, a small health bar (shield over health) floating above the target's head that follows them, with one damage number that counts up as you keep hitting (9, 18, 27...) and pops on each hit (yellow for headshots, red for the kill); both fade about 2 s after your last hit, a red hurt flash, health and shield bars, the death screen with respawn countdown, a kill feed, remote players hidden when dead and see-through while protected. Practice dummies exist only offline (the range's targets, M10, are server-side).

## How it was checked
- Go: 27 combat tests (damage order, kill flow and heal, spawn protection, shield recharge, respawn, spawn choice with and without line of sight, event resending, **lag compensation with a moving target and the rewind cap**) plus the zero-allocation rule through kills and respawns, and the race detector. Sabotage checks: disabling the rewind, removing the cap, and letting protected players take damage were each caught by the right test.
- Weapon prediction under 160 ms round trip with jitter and 3% loss: no corrections.
- `pnpm combatcheck`: two headless bots, built from the real client classes, fight through the real server over the 80 ms / 3% fake network, each aiming perfectly at where the other appears on its own screen. With lag compensation 92% of shots register (6 kills, 6 deaths, every kill in both kill feeds); with it switched off only 22% do.

## Decisions made on the way (change if you disagree)
- **Rewind cap is 333 ms, not 250 ms.** At 80 ms each way a shooter's screen is about 260 ms behind the server (160 ms round trip + 100 ms interpolation), so 250 ms would clip the exact case you are testing. Players with worse than about 80 ms one-way will have to lead their targets.
- **Hit markers wait for the server** (about a round trip). Tracers, muzzle flash and recoil are immediate. A predicted hit marker would sometimes lie.
- **The first spawn also has protection**, and protected players soak up bullets rather than letting them pass.
- **Weapon time is input-driven too**: reloads and cooldowns advance only with a player's inputs, which keeps prediction exact.

## Known limits
- A shot fired just after the target dies (before the shooter's screen shows it) cannot register; the check counts and excludes those.
- Weapon and kill statistics are tracked on the server (kills, deaths) but there is no scoreboard yet (M6).
- Spawn protection ends only by time or firing; no spawn-camping rules beyond choosing hidden, far spawns.
- Rewind assumes the client's `viewTick` is honest within the cap. There is no cheat detection beyond clamping.
