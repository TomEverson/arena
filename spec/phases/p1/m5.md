# M5. Networked combat

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** not started · **Depends on:** M4, M2

## Scope
- [ ] Server-side hit detection with lag compensation (about 250 ms position history, rewind to the shooter's view)
- [ ] Health + shield with out-of-combat recharge; damage and headshots
- [ ] Death, respawn after 3 s, spawn point farthest from enemies and out of line of sight, 2 s spawn protection (ends on fire)
- [ ] Kill heals health and shield to full
- [ ] Kill feed

## Done when
Two players can fight; hits register fairly at 80 ms; dying and respawning works.

## Notes
Use fake latency from the first commit of this milestone; lag compensation is easy to get almost right.
