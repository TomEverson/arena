# M12. FFA map

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** third layout built (200 x 200 m, greybox, unnamed), awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M1, M6, M10, M11.

## Why
FFA rooms were running the movement test arena. A real map should show off the movement kit (wall run, wall jump, mantle, slide, dash) and suit 8 players in 6 minutes.

## What was built
`levels/ffa.json`, 200 x 200 m, generated by `node levels/gen-ffa.mjs` (edit the layout there).

- **First layout (60 x 60 m):** a flat floor with separate pieces standing on it. It read as plain, with every high place a dead end.
- **Second layout (60 x 60 m):** three heights and connected routes (the table below). It played well but was far too small: at 11 to 13 m/s you cross 60 m in about five seconds.
- **Third layout (200 x 200 m):** the second layout became the centre district, with eight districts around it on a 3 x 3 grid (66 m apart), 8 m avenues between them and a ring road inside the perimeter wall. Each outer district is written once and placed twice, the second time mirrored.

| Outer district | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Blocks | North-west, south-east | Four buildings on streets: a 7 m block with a passage, a 6 m room with two doors, a 9 m block with a ledge, a 4 m building with a ramp; a skybridge between two roofs |
| Yard | North-east, south-west | Five staggered rows of 14 m containers, 3 m high and some stacked to 6 m, under an 11 m crane |
| Basin | North, south | A 44 x 28 m basin sunk 3 m, with islands, a ramp at each end and a bridge; kerbs along the long sides; a 5 m hall with a passage on each bank |
| Terraces | West, east | Three steps (2, 4, 6 m) with a ramp, a 16 m tower, and an alley between two 9 m walls for wall running |
| Avenues | Between districts | Twenty-four gates: a walkway at 5 m across the avenue (joining the districts' high routes) with a 2.5 m wall beside it across half the avenue; an 8 m pillar where avenues cross |
| Ring road | Inside the perimeter | 3.5 m blocks, alternately against the wall and against the districts |

The centre district:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Pit (-3 m) | 20 x 20 m sunken square in the middle, with cover. Out by a ramp (north-east, south-west), a tunnel west that comes up a ramp in an open trench, or a mantle onto the ledge around the edge |
| Centre | Three storeys: the pit, a cross of bridges at ground level with a column where they cross, and the crown (3.5 m platform on four pillars: jump at a pillar from a bridge and mantle up) |
| Pit rim | 2.2 m kerbs a metre back from the edge: from the ground you see along the bridges, not across the pit |
| High loop (4 to 7 m) | North deck, east deck (ramps at the north-west and south-east ends, an arcade underneath), south-east roof, south gantry, room roof, west catwalks, north-west roof. Every gap is a jump, a double jump or a mantle |
| North-west | Block with a passage through it |
| North-east | Two 10 m walls 4 m apart: wall run and kick across |
| South-east | Block with a low tunnel (1.4 m clear) to slide through |
| South-west | Room with two doors; its roof is reached from the catwalk |
| Steps | A 2.5 m step beside the north catwalk and a 3 m step beside the gantry: mantle onto the step, then jump and mantle onto the walkway (these replaced climbable posts when climbing was removed) |
| Ring | 3.5 m blocks in the open stretches, lower walls and crates |
| Everywhere | 26 m perimeter walls (the first 16 m ones could be climbed from the room roof with wall jumps and a mantle; a random-input sweep now tops out at 18 m); 8 spawn pads in the centre, each under the deck, in the room or behind a wall, and two in every outer district (24 in all) |

- The server's FFA rooms load it (`--level` defaults to `ffa.json`); practice rooms keep the greybox course.
- The welcome message now names each room's own map, and the client loads the map of that name (`client/src/levels.ts`), so prediction always uses the server's geometry.

## Checked
- Server: the level loads, has 8 spawns with standing room at each, and no practice targets; a welcome names the room's own map, per mode.
- Client: tests for bounds, perimeter height, standing at every pad, a wall run in the corridor, the steps up to the catwalk and gantry, the tunnel into the pit and a mantle out, the ramps up to both decks, and a flood fill showing every pad can walk to the bridges.
- Sightlines, measured between random standing spots at eye height on the 200 m map. On the ground: 56% of pairs under 25 m apart see each other, 21% at 25 to 50 m, 7% at 50 to 100 m, 2% beyond 100 m. Counting roofs and walkways: 56%, 31%, 15%, 7%. The longest ground view is 194 m, down an avenue.
- Sweeps in the movement sim on the 200 m map: 1.26 million steps of random full-kit input from 38 start points (no teleports, no clipping into geometry, highest point reached 15 m against 26 m walls), and a run, jump and strafe into every side of every piece (nothing flagged).
- Each link of the high loop was run in the movement sim (jump, double jump or mantle as listed).
- A seeded random run on the map is a shared test vector (`fuzz-ffa`), so the Go server and the TypeScript reference agree on this geometry.

## Not done
- No name, theme, landmarks or lighting yet; it is all greybox boxes.
- Not playtested with several players. Open questions: is 200 m too big for 8 players (the spawn chooser picks the pad farthest from enemies, so a respawn can be 100 m from the fight), are the decks and roofs too strong for the sniper, is the pit worth going into.
- The level renderer makes six materials per box: at 338 boxes that is about 2,000 draw calls. Merge them if the frame rate drops.
