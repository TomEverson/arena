# M12. FFA map

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** second layout built (greybox, unnamed), awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M1, M6, M10, M11.

## Why
FFA rooms were running the movement test arena. A real map should show off the movement kit (wall run, wall jump, climb, mantle, slide, dash) and suit 8 players in 6 minutes.

## What was built
`levels/ffa.json`, 60 x 60 m, generated by `node levels/gen-ffa.mjs` (edit the layout there). The first layout was a flat floor with separate pieces standing on it (a ring of lanes around a raised centre); it read as plain, with every high place a dead end. The second layout has three heights and connected routes:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Pit (-3 m) | 20 x 20 m sunken square in the middle, with cover. Out by a ramp (north-east, south-west), a tunnel west that comes up a ramp in an open trench, the climbable pillars, or a mantle onto the ledge around the edge |
| Centre | Three storeys: the pit, a cross of bridges at ground level with a column where they cross, and the crown (3.5 m platform on four climbable pillars) |
| Pit rim | 2.2 m kerbs a metre back from the edge: from the ground you see along the bridges, not across the pit |
| High loop (4 to 7 m) | North deck, east deck (ramps at the north-west and south-east ends, an arcade underneath), south-east roof, south gantry, room roof, west catwalks, north-west roof. Every gap is a jump, a double jump or a mantle |
| North-west | Block with a passage through it |
| North-east | Two 10 m walls 4 m apart: wall run and kick across |
| South-east | Block with a low tunnel (1.4 m clear) to slide through |
| South-west | Room with two doors and a climbable wall up to its roof |
| Ring | 3.5 m blocks in the open stretches, lower walls and crates |
| Everywhere | 26 m perimeter walls (the first 16 m ones could be climbed from the room roof with wall jumps and a mantle; a random-input sweep now tops out at 18 m); 8 spawn pads, each under the deck, in the room or behind a wall |

- The server's FFA rooms load it (`--level` defaults to `ffa.json`); practice rooms keep the greybox course.
- The welcome message now names each room's own map, and the client loads the map of that name (`client/src/levels.ts`), so prediction always uses the server's geometry.

## Checked
- Server: the level loads, has 8 spawns with standing room at each, and no practice targets; a welcome names the room's own map, per mode.
- Client: tests for bounds, perimeter height, standing at every pad, a wall run in the corridor, climbing the room wall, the tunnel into the pit and a mantle out, the ramps up to both decks, and a flood fill showing every pad can walk to the bridges.
- Sightlines, measured between random standing spots at eye height: on the ground 17% of pairs see each other (6% of pairs over 25 m apart), against 26% (10%) in the first layout. Counting the high routes too it is 32% (26%) against 33% (16%): being up high is exposed on purpose.
- Each link of the high loop was run in the movement sim (jump, double jump or mantle as listed).
- A seeded random run on the map is a shared test vector (`fuzz-ffa`), so the Go server and the TypeScript reference agree on this geometry.

## Not done
- No name, theme, landmarks or lighting yet; it is all greybox boxes.
- Not playtested with several players. Open questions: is 60 m the right size, are the decks too strong for the sniper, is the pit worth going into.
