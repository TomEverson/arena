# M12. FFA map

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** first layout built (greybox, unnamed), awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M1, M6, M10, M11.

## Why
FFA rooms were running the movement test arena. A real map should show off the movement kit (wall run, wall jump, climb, mantle, slide, dash) and suit 8 players in 6 minutes.

## What was built
`levels/ffa.json`, 60 x 60 m, generated by `node levels/gen-ffa.mjs` (edit the layout there). A ring of lanes around a raised centre:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Centre | 3 m platform with a ramp north and south, two climbable pillars east and west, low cover on top |
| North | Corridor between two 10 m walls 4 m apart: wall run and kick across |
| South | Low tunnel (1.3 m clear) to slide through, with a roof to run over |
| West | Climbable 10 m tower, a 2 m wall to mantle, a 4 m ledge with a step |
| East | Two 3 m platforms 8 m apart (dash or double jump across), a ramp up to each |
| Everywhere | Cover boxes between the lanes; 16 m perimeter walls (too tall to wall-jump out of); 8 spawn pads (four corners, four mid-edge) |

- The server's FFA rooms load it (`--level` defaults to `ffa.json`); practice rooms keep the greybox course.
- The welcome message now names each room's own map, and the client loads the map of that name (`client/src/levels.ts`), so prediction always uses the server's geometry.

## Checked
- Server: the level loads, has 8 spawns with standing room at each, and no practice targets; a welcome names the room's own map, per mode.
- Client: tests for bounds, perimeter height, standing at every pad, a wall run in the corridor, climbing the tower, and every pad running toward the centre without leaving the map or sticking.
- A seeded random run on the map is a shared test vector (`fuzz-ffa`), so the Go server and the TypeScript reference agree on this geometry.

## Not done
- No name, theme, landmarks or lighting yet; it is all greybox boxes.
- Not playtested with several players. Open questions: is 60 m the right size, are the lanes too different, is the centre too strong.
- Spawn pads are used as single points; the server does not yet pick a random spot inside a pad.
