# M1. Local movement feel (no network)

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M0 (done)

## Scope
- [x] Level loader: `levels/*.json` (boxes, ramps) renders and builds collision geometry
- [x] Pure movement function in `client/src/sim/movement.ts`: `(state, input, dt) -> state`, float64, no engine physics
- [x] Walk, jump, slide with momentum, 2-charge regenerating dash, double jump, mantle, climbing (hold Space facing a climbable wall; no sprint key: base speed is already fast, Ultrakill-style)
- [x] Collision against boxes and ramps (swept AABB or similar), deterministic
- [x] Greybox test arena with vertical spaces to exercise the moves
- [x] Unit tests for each move (these become test vectors in M3)

## Done when
It feels good to move around for 10 minutes, and the function is pure and deterministic.

## Where things are
- `client/src/sim/`: `movement.ts` (`stepPlayer`), `params.ts` (all tuning numbers), `collision.ts`, `level.ts` (loader), `types.ts`
- `levels/greybox.json`: test arena (step, crates, 2 m mantle wall, 4 m wall, platform stairs, 3 m ramp, slide tunnel, 9 m dash gap, pillar; climbable walls are tinted green: the 4 m wall, the 4 m pillar and a 10 m tower at the west side)
- `client/src/main.ts`: player controller. Keys: WASD move, Space jump, Shift dash, C slide, R respawn
- Tests: `movement.test.ts`, `level.test.ts` (51 passing)

## Playtest checklist (for the author)
- [ ] Run, stop, strafe feel responsive (run speed 11, instant-ish accel)
- [ ] Slide boost and slide-jump keep momentum; tunnel forces you low and you can crawl out
- [ ] Dash (2 charges, about 1.2 s regen each) crosses the 9 m gap with a jump
- [ ] Double jump feels right (one extra jump, refilled on landing)
- [ ] Climbing: hold Space facing a green wall, climb at 5 m/s, pull over the top automatically, release to fall
- [ ] Mantle works on the 2 m wall and not the 4 m wall
- [ ] Ramps are smooth up and down (no hopping); 0.3 m step does not stop you
- [ ] Tune numbers in `params.ts`, then re-run tests

## Not done / known limits
- Not yet checked by a human in a focused browser: the automation tab could not run the frame loop (hidden tab).
- No slope gravity or sliding down ramps; slide direction is fixed at start; no wall-jump, no climb stamina or time limit, no ladders (climbable is a per-box flag).
- Tuning is a first guess. When it feels right, freeze it and record vectors in M3.

## Notes
Every later milestone (vectors, prediction, lag compensation) depends on this function. Changing it late is the most expensive change. The fly camera was removed.
