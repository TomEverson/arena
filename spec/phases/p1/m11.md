# M11. Wall run and wall jump

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M1 (movement), M4 (prediction, shared test vectors). Independent of M7-M9 (parked).

## Why
Movement is meant to feel like Ultrakill and Overwatch: fast, with routes through the map. Sprint, slide, dash, double jump, climb and mantle exist; wall running and wall jumping chain them into long, readable lines through a level.

## Rules (decided)
- **Which walls:** every solid vertical box face that is not climbable. Green climbable walls keep their climbing; no wall running or kicking off them. Perimeter walls are 16 m tall so wall jumps cannot be used to leave the arena.
- **Start a wall run:** in the air, at least 0.6 m above the floor, not crouching or sliding, with a wall within 12 cm on any side, moving **along** the wall at 6 m/s or more, and holding a direction that follows the wall.
- **While running:** speed along the wall is at least 11 m/s (run speed), gravity is 15%, a jump's upward speed is cut to 3 m/s on grabbing, and a small push of 2 m/s presses the player to the wall. Lasts at most **1.5 s**. Ends when: time is up, speed along the wall falls under 4 m/s, the direction keys are released, the slide key is pressed, a dash starts, the wall ends, the player lands or mantles.
- **Wall jump:** press jump while wall running, or in the air while touching any such wall. The player is kicked **8 m/s away from the wall and up at 9.5 m/s**, keeping their speed along the wall, and **one air jump is given back**. At most **3** per time in the air (resets on landing).
- **Re-grab cooldown:** the wall just left cannot be grabbed or kicked off again for 0.3 s, so kicking between two walls works but one wall cannot be climbed by bouncing.
- **Feel:** the camera banks about 4 degrees away from the wall; a scrape sound plays while running and a thwack on the kick; the character sprints with arms out wide (other players see it through a new `wallRunning` entity flag).

## Found while building
- Wall jumping straight into a wall face lets a player kick up it and then mantle over: three kicks plus mantle reach about 8 m. The 8 m perimeter walls were climbable out of the arena, hence 16 m. The `mantle-too-high` scenario now uses a 7 m wall.
- A jump's upward speed carried straight up the wall (a 6 m climb) until it was cut on grabbing.
- `EntityFlags` had all 8 bits used. It is now 16 bits; the generator picks the Go type from the largest bit.
