# M13. Projectile weapons

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M2 (weapon), M5 (lag compensation), M11/M12 (the map to fight on).

> **Offline mode was removed on 2026-10-13.** The client no longer simulates the world on its own (no `?offline`, no client-only dummies, no local bullets): without a server it shows a message. The practice range is an online room. Mentions of offline play below describe what was built at the time.

## Why
The rifle was hitscan: the server drew an instant line and applied damage the same tick. Apex-style fights want travel time: you lead moving targets, long shots drop. Most weapons should be projectiles; a few (a sniper, say) stay hitscan, so both paths exist.

## How it works
- **Bullet physics** (`client/src/sim/bullet.ts`, Go twin `server/internal/game/bullet.go`): a bullet leaves the eye at `bulletSpeed` (300 m/s) and falls at `bulletGravity` (8 m/s²). Each step moves it and casts the **whole segment travelled** against the level and the target boxes, so a 5 m-per-tick bullet cannot tunnel through a thin wall or a player. Range is `weapon.range` (250 m).
- **Server** (`server/internal/game/bullets.go`): a shot spawns a bullet; the world flies every bullet each tick against everyone but its owner, and applies damage the tick it lands (`applyHit`). Hitscan (`resolveShot`) is kept for weapons that need it.
- **Lag compensation:** the shooter's screen showed the world `lag` ticks in the past. At spawn the bullet is flown `lag` steps at once, each against the targets as the shooter saw them at that moment (the same history as before, capped at 20 ticks); after that it has caught up with real time and flies against live positions. Walls never move, so only targets need rewinding.
- **Tracers for everyone:** each shot is announced in snapshots (`shots`: origin, direction, tick, resent for 6 ticks). Your own bullet is drawn the instant you fire; other players' bullets start when your screen reaches the moment they fired. The picture is only that: the server decides hits, and hit markers still come from events. Other players' gunfire now also makes a sound, placed where it was fired.
- **Offline practice** flies its own bullets against the local dummies.

## Parameters (`shared/params.json`, `weapon`)
`bulletSpeed` 300, `bulletGravity` 8 (the range is the existing `range`). At 300 m/s the bullet crosses the 60 m FFA map in 0.2 s, so a runner needs about 2 m of lead from the far side; the drop at 60 m is about 15 cm.

## Checked
- Bullet vectors (9, recorded from TypeScript, replayed by Go): flight and drop, range, wall, thin wall, floor, body, head, a moving target led and not, a slower bullet.
- Go tests: travel time (a 45 m shot lands about nine ticks later), drop, dodging a sprinter, tracers announced then dropped, walls stop bullets, range ends them, and zero allocations with bullets flying (race detector clean). The earlier lag-compensation tests still pass with bullets.
- End to end: through 80 ms and 3% loss the bots (which now lead and compensate for drop) hit 90% (duel) and 88% (range) with lag compensation and about 18-20% without, so the catch-up works.

## Not done
- One weapon only. A second, hitscan weapon is the next step when the weapon set grows; `resolveShot` and the hitscan vectors are ready for it.
- Bullets are points (no hit radius), like hitscan. Bullet speed and drop are untuned: playtest them.
- A bullet passing through a spot where a player *was* only counts if the shooter's screen showed them there; very high ping (over about 330 ms) is clamped.
