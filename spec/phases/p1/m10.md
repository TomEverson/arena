# M10. Game modes: Practice Range + FFA

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M6 (rooms), M5 (combat), M2 (practice dummies). Independent of M7-M9 (parked).

> **Climbing was removed on 2026-10-13** (after M12): wall jumps plus a mantle reached everything a climbable wall did, faster. The `climbable` level flag, the `jumpHeld` input, the `climbing` state and the green stripes are gone; mentions of climbing below describe what was built at the time.

> **Offline mode was removed on 2026-10-13.** The client no longer simulates the world on its own (no `?offline`, no client-only dummies, no local bullets): without a server it shows a message. The practice range is an online room. Mentions of offline play below describe what was built at the time.

## Why
Today there is one mode, the FFA match, plus an offline fallback with client-only dummies. Tuning movement and gunplay needs a place with no match clock, no opponents who shoot back, and real server-side hit registration. So a room gets a **mode**:

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **FFA** | The current game: wait for players, countdown, 6-minute match, scoreboard, results, rematch. Unchanged. |
| **Practice range** | An always-live sandbox: the movement course and shooting targets, no timer or scoreboard, targets that do not shoot back, and a stats panel to measure time-to-kill and accuracy. Solo, or share the link to practise with a friend. |

## What already exists to build on
- The server's `--practice` flag (rooms that are always live) and `MatchConfig.Enabled`.
- The greybox arena: the movement course (step, crates, 2 m wall, platforms, ramp, slide tunnel, dash gap, climbable walls) and `targets[]` in `levels/greybox.json` (3 static, 2 strafing). The TypeScript level loader reads them; the Go one ignores them.
- Client-only dummies (`dummies.ts`, `dummyView.ts`, `hitFeedback.ts`) used in offline mode. Their hits are not server-validated, so they cannot test lag compensation.

## Scope
- [x] **Room modes**: `ffa` and `practice`. `POST /api/rooms` takes a mode; the welcome message and snapshots say which mode the room is in; each mode chooses its level and rules (match cycle on or off). A room link keeps its mode.
- [x] **Start screen**: opening the page with no room link offers "Play FFA" and "Practice range" (and `?mode=practice` as a shortcut). Opening a room link skips the menu. A way back to the menu.
- [x] **Server-side targets**: the world runs the level's targets (static and strafing dummies) at 60 Hz. They have health and shield, can be hit and killed, and respawn after about 2 s. They are in snapshots as entities, have position history so lag compensation works on moving ones, and produce the same hit, kill and health-bar events as players.
- [x] **Practice rules**: always live, no countdown, timer, results or scoreboard; spawn protection off; targets never shoot back; players cannot damage each other (so friends can share the range).
- [x] **Practice stats panel**: last and average time-to-kill, shots, hits, accuracy, headshot share, damage dealt; a key to reset the stats and the targets.
- [x] **Testing helpers** (small, practice only): refill health and ammo with a key; teleport back to the course start. (Further tools are listed under Decisions.)
- [x] **Offline fallback stays** for when no server is running; it keeps using the client-only dummies.
- [x] **Checks**: Go tests (target simulation, lag compensation on a strafing target, kill and respawn, no player-vs-player damage in practice, mode in the rooms API); TypeScript tests for the menu helpers and the stats maths; `pnpm practicecheck` (a bot shooting strafing targets through the fake network, with and without lag compensation).

## What was built
- **Modes**: `POST /api/rooms {"mode": "ffa" | "practice"}`; the welcome message and room info carry the mode; `Rooms` builds each mode from a `ModeConfig` (level, match rules, practice flag). Server flags: `--practice-level` (default greybox), and `--no-match` (renamed from `--practice`) for always-live FFA rooms.
- **Start screen**: opening the page without a room link shows "Play FFA" and "Practice range"; `?mode=practice` or `?mode=ffa` skips it; a room link joins straight in. The address bar then holds the room link to share. "no server? play offline" stays.
- **Server targets** (`server/internal/game/targets.go`): the level's `targets[]` become dummies the world moves at 60 Hz (static or strafing), with health and shield, position history for lag compensation, kill and respawn after 2 s. They are entities in snapshots (`kind: target`, ids from 1000), draw as the orange-and-red dummies, flash white when hit, and give the usual health bar and damage numbers.
- **Practice rules**: always live, no clock, countdown, results or scoreboard; no spawn protection; everyone starts at the course start; bullets pass through other players and players cannot damage each other, so friends can share a range.
- **Practice panel and helpers**: shots, hits, accuracy, headshots, damage, kills and time-to-kill (last, average, best) on the left. **K** resets targets and stats, **H** refills health and ammo, **T** returns to the course start. The same panel and keys work in offline mode against the local dummies.
- **Refactor**: lag compensation now goes through one `history` type shared by players and targets.

## How it was checked
- Go: 14 practice tests (targets from the level and in snapshots, none in FFA rooms, strafing range, hit and health reporting, headshots, kill and respawn, **lag compensation on a strafing target**, no player-vs-player damage and bullets passing through players, no spawn protection, the three helpers, zero allocations with targets dying and respawning), level-loader tests for targets, and WebSocket tests for modes (create, welcome, refused unknown mode, room info, helper commands over the wire). Sabotage checks (no rewind on targets, players able to hurt each other, FFA rooms growing targets) were each caught by a test.
- TypeScript: practice stats maths, mode-from-URL, target names; 256 client tests in total.
- `pnpm practicecheck`: through the 80 ms / 3% fake network, a bot shooting the range's targets hits 93% with lag compensation and 21% without; a second player firing at the first never hurt them; refill, course start and target reset all worked over the wire. FFA checks (`netcheck`, `matchcheck`, `combatcheck`) still pass.

## Decisions made on the way (change if you disagree)
- **Practice players start at the first spawn point** (the course start), not spread out.
- **The practice level is `--practice-level`** (default the greybox arena); `levels/range.json` is a clean shooting range used by the check.
- **Targets use no team**: `teamId` 0, ids from 1000.
- **Kills of targets do not heal you or count on a scoreboard.**

## Known limits
- Targets only stand or strafe in a line: no jumping, ducking or shooting back.
- The panel's numbers are per browser session and reset with K (they are not stored by the server).
- No tuning panel, adjustable target speed, infinite ammo or slow-motion (decided against for now).

## Proposed build order (as planned)
1. Protocol: room `mode`, a `target` entity flag; level loader reads `targets[]` in Go.
2. Server: target simulation and history, hitting and killing them, practice rules, per-mode room config.
3. Rooms API and welcome carry the mode.
4. Client: start screen, practice HUD (stats panel, reset, helpers), target visuals (reusing the dummy look).
5. Practice check, docs, playtest.

## Done when
From the start screen you can pick Practice range or FFA. In the range you can run the movement course and shoot server-side targets, hits register correctly at 160 ms round trip (including on strafing targets), and you can read your time-to-kill and accuracy. A friend can join the same range by link. FFA behaves exactly as before.

## Decisions needed (proposed defaults in bold)
1. **How to choose a mode**: **a start screen with two buttons** (plus `?mode=practice`), or two URLs only.
2. **Players in the range**: **multiplayer allowed, no player-vs-player damage**, or strictly solo.
3. **Targets**: **simulated on the server** (real hit registration, lag compensation), keeping the client-only dummies for offline only.
4. **Which level for FFA**: **greybox for both**, or a different arena for FFA (for example the open duel arena, or a new map later).
5. **Extra testing tools** beyond the proposed helpers: **none for now**, or pick from: live tuning panel for movement and weapon numbers, adjustable target speed and distance, infinite ammo toggle, slow-motion, bots as opponents (that one overlaps with M7).
