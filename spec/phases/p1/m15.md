# M15. Weapon slots and the sniper

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M2, M5, M13 (bullets), M14 (melee).

## The slots
| Key | Weapon | |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Brick**, the rifle | a projectile weapon (M13); the default, and what you respawn with |
| **2** | **Needle**, the sniper | **hitscan**: it hits the instant it is fired, along the aim line |
| **3** | empty hands | no gun; the punch still works |

## Switching
- Takes **0.4 s** between guns, **0.2 s** to or from empty hands (`switchTime`, `switchQuickTime`). You cannot fire or reload during the draw; melee works.
- The old gun's reload is lost. **Each gun keeps its own rounds** (`ammo` is the gun in hand, `stash` the other; `gun` says which is which, so empty hands remember the last gun).
- The gun in hand shows to everyone (`holdingSniper` / `emptyHanded` entity flags): other bodies hold the right model, or empty hands. Taking out a gun plays the draw animation.
- Practice "refill" tops up both guns and keeps the weapon in hand.

## The Needle (`shared/params.json`, `sniper`)
| | |
|---|---|
| Damage | 75 body, 150 headshot (a full health and shield bar is 150: a headshot on a full bar kills) |
| Fire rate | one shot every 1.4 s (a bolt cycle you can see and hear) |
| Magazine | 5 rounds; reload 2.6 s |
| Aim | hold right mouse for a **4x scope**: the whole view zooms in 4x, the gun and its scope stay on screen with the scope's axis at the middle (you look through its hollow tube, thin reticle at the eyepiece), and the cone is pinpoint. No black mask. Hip spread is large (4 degrees) |
| Range | 500 m; no drop, no travel time |
| Lag compensation | the same rewind as before: aimed at where your screen showed the target, it hits |

## How it is built
- **One weapon table, two guns:** `WEAPON_DEFS` (TypeScript, `client/src/sim/weapon.ts`) and `weaponDefs` (Go, `server/internal/game/weapon.go`) hold what differs per gun. `stepWeapon` is the same code for both, driven by the weapon state's `slot`; switching, per-gun ammo and the draw lock all live there, so the client predicts them exactly. New weapon vectors cover switching and the sniper's cycle.
- **Server:** a shot from a hitscan gun goes through `resolveShot` (rewound, instant); a rifle shot spawns a bullet (M13). Both announce the shot (`gun` says which) so everyone draws the right tracer: a bullet, or a near-instant streak for the sniper.
- **Viewmodel and body:** `client/src/sniper.ts` builds the Needle with the Brick's interface (ammo strip, bolt, magazine), so the first-person view, the third-person body and the viewer treat both alike. The viewer's weapon selector has Needle, and so does its model selector.
- **HUD:** the ammo counter follows the gun in hand (or says EMPTY HANDS) and a 1/2/3 strip shows the active slot.

## The gun keeps its size when the world zooms
The first-person gun is drawn by its own camera with a fixed field of view, on top of the world (`viewScene` and `viewCamera` in `render.ts`), so a 4x scope zoom does not make the gun four times bigger. The same goes for the rifle's aim-down-sights.

## Movement depends on the weapon
- **Aiming slowdown is per gun:** the rifle keeps 0.65 of run speed while aiming, the sniper only **0.35** (`adsSpeedMult`).
- **Empty hands run faster:** 1.15 of run speed (`emptyRunMult`), on the ground and in the air. Slides and dashes are unchanged.
- **How:** movement itself does not know the weapon, so both sides work the multipliers out from the weapon state before each step (`movementMods`) and put them on the input (`runSpeedMult`, `adsSpeedMult`). The server **recomputes** them and ignores whatever a client sends, so a tampered client cannot run faster.
- **Left click with empty hands punches:** there is no gun to fire, so the fire button is melee (V still works too).
