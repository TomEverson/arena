# M2. Local gunplay

**Phase:** 1 (FFA Deathmatch) · **Status:** code complete, awaiting playtest · **Depends on:** M1

## Scope
- [x] Assault rifle: hitscan, fire rate, ammo, reload
- [x] ADS (zoom, slower movement), hipfire spread
- [x] Fixed per-gun recoil pattern players can learn
- [x] Hit feedback: crosshair, hit markers, hitstop, screenshake; placeholder viewmodel
- [x] Dummy targets with 100 health + 50 shield, headshot multiplier
- [ ] Tune movement and time-to-kill together (needs your playtest)

## Done when
Time-to-kill on dummies is about 1.5-2 s (150 total health) and recoil is learnable.

## Where things are
- `client/src/sim/weapon.ts`: fire rate, ammo, reload, spread, recoil pattern, damage (shield then health), view direction. Pure and seeded; ported to Go in M5.
- `client/src/sim/hitscan.ts`: ray vs boxes, ramps and target hitboxes; headshot = hit at or above 1.5 m.
- `client/src/sim/params.ts`: `WEAPON` block holds every number (damage, rpm, spread, recoil table, ADS).
- `client/src/dummies.ts`, `dummyView.ts`: practice targets from `levels/greybox.json` (`targets[]`: 3 static at 12/26/44 m, 2 strafing).
- `client/src/fx.ts`: crosshair, hit markers, screenshake, placeholder rifle, tracers, impact flashes.
- Tests: `weapon.test.ts` (fire control, recoil, spread, damage, TTK, hitscan), `dummies.test.ts`, plus ADS tests in `movement.test.ts` (81 total).

## Controls
LMB fire (hold) · RMB aim (hold) · R reload · P respawn (moved from R) · plus the M1 keys.

## Current numbers (first guess)
AR 600 rpm, 9 damage (13.5 head), 30 round mag, 1.8 s reload. 100 health + 50 shield. Perfect-aim time to kill on a full-health target: about 1.6 s body, about 1.1 s head (asserted in tests: 1.4-2.0 s body).
Hipfire cone 2 degrees (x0.6 while crouched, x0.85 while sliding, with only 30% of the movement bloom), ADS 0.15 degrees; bloom from movement, air time and sustained fire. ADS: 0.7x FOV, 0.65x ground speed, 0.7x look sensitivity.
Recoil: 30-entry pitch/yaw table, resets 0.35 s after you stop firing; the view itself is kicked, you pull down to counter.

## Playtest checklist (for the author)
- [ ] Can you learn the recoil and hold the crosshair on a static dummy at 26 m for a full mag?
- [ ] Is TTK in practice about 1.5-2 s? (HUD shows "last kill" time, shots, hits, headshots)
- [ ] Strafing dummies: hittable while you also strafe/slide, or does movement make aiming impossible? (Tune hipfire/bloom or movement.)
- [ ] Hit markers, kill marker and shake feel readable but not noisy
- [ ] ADS zoom and slowdown feel right; sensitivity while aimed
- [ ] Reload length (1.8 s) and magazine (30) feel right

## Known limits
- No sound; no damage numbers; placeholder viewmodel (boxes) and no muzzle/reload animation.
- Hitstop only freezes the dummies (about 67 ms), never the player: freezing the player would fight prediction later.
- Tracers/impacts are cosmetic. Spread uses the weapon's seeded RNG; in M5 the seed must travel with the input so the server reproduces it.

## Notes
If fast movement makes dummies (strafing, sliding) too hard to hit, adjust movement or spread before moving on.
