# 01. Vision

## One-liner
A fast, Ultrakill-flavored 3D FPS that runs entirely in the browser: teams of 4-6+ fight each other and AI enemies (PvPvE) over control points.

## Decided
- **Platform:** fully playable in the browser; join by link, no install.
- **Genre:** 3D first-person shooter, multiplayer is mandatory.
- **Social base requirement:** must be fun with the author's friend group (teams of 4-6, possibly more).
- **Scale target:** 2 players for the baseline, then 32 (about 5 teams of 6), larger later.
- **Dev approach:** mostly vibe coded; no 3D modeling skills or interest.
- **Not an exact extraction shooter.** Extraction and battle-royale ideas are inspiration only.

## Design principles (derived from the friend-group requirement)
1. Nobody sits out: death costs time or resources, never the rest of the match.
2. Uneven team sizes must work (fill with bots or scale objectives).
3. Joining takes seconds (URL in group chat).
4. Everyone has a useful job beyond aim (reviving, pinging, capturing, healing).
5. Matches should produce funny, retellable moments, not just scoreboards.
6. Short matches. A long match time was an explicit complaint about extraction shooters.

## Inspirations
- Ultrakill (look, speed, blood-as-health)
- BattleBit Remastered (large-scale multiplayer on a cheap art style)
- Unturned (low-poly, accessible)
- Krunker.io, Shell Shockers, surviv.io (proof that browser multiplayer shooters work)

## Non-goals (for now)
- Persistent progression, inventory, or accounts
- Squad matchmaking or ranked play
- Mobile support
- 254-player matches
